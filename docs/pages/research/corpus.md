# Corpus study (RQ1)

How much of what developers write into `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `.cursorrules`, `GEMINI.md` and
`.windsurfrules` can a deterministic tool enforce?

Every line was labelled twice: by a deterministic regex rubric (`corpus/label.py`) and by a model, in two
independent passes with a fixed prompt. No human labelled the corpus. Both labellings are reported, because they
disagree, and the disagreement is itself a finding. The tables below are generated by `corpus/analyse.py`.
